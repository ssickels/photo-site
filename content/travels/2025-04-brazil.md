# Lençóis Maranhenses National Park, Brazil

I had an amazing vist to the otherworldy Lençóis Maranhenses National Park in Brazil. A *huge* area of sand dunes and lagoons. It's so large that it's easily visible when you're zoomed way out in Google maps. 

Great photography workshop, orgainzed and led by [Bruce Percy](https://www.brucepercy.co.uk/) -- one of my favorite photographers! With whom I've been on several workshops around the world (Scotland, and several times to South America). 

It's the light tan area along the Atlantic Coast in northern Brazil. (Click on "Show map" if you want to see it.)

For the first part of the trip, we stayed in the town of Santo Amaro do Maranhão, to the west of the park, and made our way into the park each day before sunrise via four-wheel drives over the dunes. (And sometimes through shallow-ish water!). No roads; just sand! Then back out again for sunsets. For the latter part of our trip, we moved to the east of the park, staying in the small town of Atins. Which is accessible only via small boats along the Preguiças River! 

It was all a fun -- and amazing -- adventure!
