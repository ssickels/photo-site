# Winter in Northern Norway

We had a wonderful trip to Norway in late January, 2026. The big goal/hope was to see the Aurora Borealis. And we certainly did! But the entire trip was a truly enjoyable treat.

- Tromsø: Jan 18 – 21.
- Hurtigruten’s MS Trollfjord: Jan 22 – 29. On a ship, from Tromsø to the North Cape, then south to Oslo, stopping in ports (small towns) along the way for explorations.
- Oslo: Jan 30 – Feb 3.

A week above the Arctic Circle in
January: Tromsø, Alta and the North Cape, then south to Åndalsnes. The
northern lights came out on the very first night, which produced my first
aurora photo.

(For more details, check out my blog post [here](https://www.stevesickelsphotography.com/norway/))
